A strategic and structural skill set that spans the full early-phase UX process.

UX Strategy & Information Architecture

Client Discovery & Requirements Gathering

Facilitating stakeholder workshops and interviews to surface real business goals, user needs, and constraints before a single wireframe is drawn.
