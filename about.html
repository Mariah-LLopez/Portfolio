About

UX strategist, information architect, and plain-language advocate.

What I Do

I work at the intersection of UX strategy and information architecture—helping teams structure complex websites and digital platforms that are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with business goals. My work begins before design starts: in discovery sessions, stakeholder interviews, and the foundational documents that define what a site should do and who it should serve.

Professional Focus

My focus is on the strategic and structural layer of digital projects. I specialize in building the architecture that designers and developers need to do their best work—site maps, user flows, navigation logic, content frameworks, and detailed requirements documentation. I approach every project with a user-first mindset grounded in business reality.

Skills & Approach

I bring both analytical rigor and clear communication to every engagement. I translate complex requirements into organized, documented deliverables that cross-functional teams can actually use. I care about accessibility—not as a compliance checkbox, but as a design principle. And I default to collaboration: the best IA comes from listening carefully to stakeholders, developers, and users alike.
