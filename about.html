About

Analytics engineer | Digital Products Specialist

What I Do

I design and build data-driven digital products that make complex information easier to understand and use. My work sits at the intersection of analytics, UX, and development, turning raw data into dashboards, systems, and interfaces that support real business decisions.

Professional Focus

My focus is on structuring data and systems behind the scenes while shaping how users interact with them on the front end. I work with data validation, dashboards, and documentation to ensure accuracy and clarity, while applying UX and web design principles to create intuitive and accessible experiences. I am especially interested in building scalable systems that connect business needs with user behavior.

Skills & Approach

I approach projects by starting with structure first, organizing data, defining logic, and understanding user needs, then translating that into clean and functional design. My background in technical communication helps me simplify complex systems, while my experience in analytics and development allows me to build solutions that are both practical and user-focused.
